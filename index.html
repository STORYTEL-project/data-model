STORYTEL Ontology

Storytel is the ontology of the European funded project Storytel. Its goal is to map the relationship between Full Video, Anonymized Video and Anonymized Story, re-using existing ontologies and implementing some ad-hoc classes.

Each box shows the Storytel class name (purple), its superclasses and its rdfs:label. Edges are the owl:someValuesFrom / owl:hasValue restrictions, property domains and ranges, and owl:disjointWith axioms. Click a class to highlight its relations and read its definition; scroll to zoom, drag to pan.
